Can you build workflows on HubSpot Starter?

Short answer

Partly, and HubSpot says two different things. The Sales Hub pricing page sells Starter with up to 50 workflows with restricted triggers and actions. The workflows documentation lists Professional and Enterprise only, and never mentions Starter. Marketing Starter is clearer: 10 automated actions and one simple workflow per form. Nobody publishes which triggers and actions the Starter restriction removes, so no honest answer can tell you whether a given workflow runs there.

What a spec looks like

Every spec follows the same nine sections, so a team reads one and knows how to read all of them.

  • Outcome, when to use it and when not to
  • Prerequisites: properties, lists, tiers, with the JSON to create them
  • Enrollment and re-enrollment, then every step with its parameters
  • Known pitfalls, a test plan, variants, and the YAML build spec

What breaks, and how the specs prevent it

The two official sources do not agree

The Sales Hub pricing page sells Starter with up to 50 workflows with restricted triggers and actions. The knowledge base article on creating workflows lists Marketing, Sales, Service, Data Hub, Smart CRM and Revenue Hub at Professional and Enterprise, and says nothing about Starter. Both are HubSpot. Read the second before you assume the first.

Restricted is the word that costs money

A restriction on triggers and actions is not a smaller version of the same tool. If the trigger you need is missing, the workflow cannot exist at all, and the count of 50 is irrelevant. HubSpot does not publish the list of what Starter removes.

Marketing Starter is a different thing again

Marketing Hub Starter is sold with up to 10 automated actions and one simple workflow per form. That is form follow up, not the workflows tool. Building a multi step nurture on it is not a matter of hitting a limit, it is a matter of the tool not being there.

What this bundle builds

Nothing, on its own. This page exists because a question with a price attached, should I pay for Professional to automate anything, has two official answers from the same vendor, and most of the content written about it is years out of date.

If your portal is on Professional or above, this page is not for you. Read the tier line on any spec instead.

How the pieces fit together

Start with the sources, because the order matters. The Sales Hub pricing page is a sales document and it counts workflows. The knowledge base article is an implementation document and it lists subscriptions. When they disagree, the implementation document is the one that tells you whether a button exists in your portal.

Then take the list of candidates here and test three of them, not fifty. Pick one that only sets a property, one that creates a task, and one that sends an internal email. If all three build and enrol, your Starter restriction is generous and the rest of the list is worth trying. If the trigger you need is not in the menu, you have your answer in ten minutes and no money spent.

Questions admins ask

Does HubSpot Starter include workflows?

The Sales Hub pricing page says Sales Starter includes up to 50 workflows with restricted triggers and actions. The workflows documentation lists only Professional and Enterprise. Until HubSpot publishes the restriction list, the safe reading is that Starter offers a reduced automation surface, not the workflows tool as documented.

How many workflows do you get on each tier?

HubSpot states up to 50 on Sales Starter with restrictions, and up to 300 fully customizable on Sales Professional. Marketing Starter is counted in automated actions, up to 10, not in workflows.

Which specs in this library run on Starter?

We do not know, and we will not guess. The 54 specs listed here are the ones whose steps are short enough to be worth testing: no custom code, no list actions, no sequence enrolment, no random split, six actions or fewer. If your Starter portal allows their triggers, they are the first to try.

Why does this page not just answer the question?

Because answering it properly needs a real Starter portal, and a developer test account cannot be one: HubSpot documents test accounts as free accounts with a 90 day trial of enterprise features. We would rather show you the sources than invent a list.

What should I do if I am on Starter and need real automation?

Price the upgrade against the work. Professional opens the documented workflows tool, with re-enrollment, branching, delays and the action library the specs in this catalogue are written against. The comparison page for that decision is the next one to read.
