Changelog

What shipped, and when. Specs are dated; when HubSpot changes a behaviour a spec relies on, the spec is updated and the change is logged here.

2026-09-20

  • Pricing rebuilt around portals. You own the library once, then you add portals: Category pack 39 EUR with 1 portal, Full library 149 EUR with 3, an Extra portal at 59 EUR each, Studio at 79 EUR a month for 5 new portals a calendar month (99 from 1 November, or 990 a year), Agency Lifetime at 5,990 EUR once for unlimited portals and seats, 20 seats in all.
  • A portal of a client counts exactly like a portal of your own, and a portal counts once, at its first install. Reinstalling and updating in a portal already activated stay free for ever.
  • The one-click installer is now included everywhere, with the portals shown on each plan. The +20 EUR add-on is gone.
  • The Agency licence at 449 / 599 EUR is retired and replaced by Agency Lifetime. Buyers keep what they bought, unchanged and unlimited.
  • Studio rents portals, never files, and needs a full library licence on the same email. Cancel it and everything already installed stays licensed.
  • Promotion codes now work on the category packs and the full library only, one per order, never on Studio or Agency Lifetime.
  • Request-a-spec becomes free for everyone, with no promised queue, instead of 3 a year on the agency licence.

2026-09-19

  • The library is complete: all 515 specs are published, including the 15 written for the industry kits. Nothing is dated for later.
  • Five industry kits open: insurance, real estate, staffing and recruiting, events, legal services. Each ships 40 chosen specs, 3 written for the trade, and the property schema of that trade as JSON.
  • Four collections become packs of their own: renewals and churn, duplicates and data cleanup, GDPR and consent, lifecycle and lead scoring.
  • HubSpot retired Operations Hub from its 2026 line-up. Every tier label now reads Data Hub, with the former name kept in the text so the old searches still land.
