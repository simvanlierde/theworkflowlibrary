How a spec is written

No screenshots of someone else's portal. A method, and a date on every line.

01

Public documentation first

Every behaviour is written against the public HubSpot documentation and ten years of implementation work, never against a client portal.

02

Every property declared

Each property used in the steps appears in the prerequisites with its label, internal name, type and options, so nothing is missing when you start building.

03

Uncertainty marked inline

Where a behaviour could not be confirmed against the current UI, the spec says so on the line rather than guessing.

FAQ

The objections first

Which HubSpot tier do I need?

Every spec states its minimum hub and tier. Most need Professional, because Starter only ships simple automation. Anything that needs Operations Hub (format data, custom code) or Enterprise is labelled as such.

Which licence do I need?

Building on your own company's portals: category pack or full library. Building on client portals, or handing specs to clients as part of your deliverables: the agency licence. Reselling or republishing the specs themselves is never covered.

Refunds?

30 days, no questions asked. Email hello@theworkflowlibrary.com with your order number.

Can I upgrade from a category pack to the full library?

Yes, for the difference. Your receipt page shows a one-time code worth what you paid, valid 90 days on the full library.

Do I pay VAT?

Prices are shown excluding VAT. Businesses tick "buying as a business" at checkout and add their VAT number: EU reverse charge applies. Individuals in the EU pay their local VAT on top. Stripe (merchant of record) issues the invoice and shows your local currency.

Is this an app or a set of templates I import?

Both, now. Every spec is documentation precise enough to build the workflow yourself in 20 to 60 minutes without giving anyone access. On top of that, the one-click installer can create the properties and the workflow for you through the Automation API, through the portal owner's own authorisation screen.

How does the one-click install work?

You buy, and the receipt page gives you a signed install link. The same link is in your Stripe receipt email, so you never need an account or a password. The link opens the authorisation screen where you pick the portal. For each spec it creates the missing properties and the workflow itself, switched off, then adds one task listing what the API cannot create for you, such as a marketing email, a team or a static list. You read the workflow, adjust the owners and the copy, switch it on. The full library covers up to 3 of your own portals, the agency licence covers unlimited client portals, a category pack can add one portal for +20 EUR. The 10 free specs install too, against an email.

How are the specs written?

From public HubSpot documentation and ten years of implementation work. Where a behaviour could not be confirmed against the current UI, the spec says so inline rather than guessing.

How often do new specs ship?

30 every Friday, 3 per category, until the catalogue of 500 is complete (25 December 2026). Each batch is dated in the changelog. Buyers of a pack receive the new specs of that pack at no extra cost.

What do I get after paying?

A download with PDF and Markdown versions of every spec in your pack, the properties JSON, the workflow JSON and the custom code, plus web access. New specs added to your pack land in the same download link every Friday.

Why do prices go up on 1 November?

The library is 50 specs today and 500 by 25 December 2026. Founding buyers pay for what is live plus everything to come. Once the library passes 250 specs the price reflects the finished library: 59 / 199 / 599 EUR. The date is fixed and the changelog records it.
