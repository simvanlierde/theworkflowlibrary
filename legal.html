Terms, licence and refunds

Licence

Not for resale, republication, or delivery to third parties as a deliverable. Agency licence. Licensed to the buyer (a freelancer or an agency, any number of people) for use on any client portal, unlimited. The buyer may hand a client the spec, the properties JSON and the code built for that client; the PDFs and Markdown in the agency pack carry no The Workflow Library branding for that purpose. Reselling, republishing or distributing the library itself, in whole or in part, as a product is not permitted.

Request-a-spec (agency). 3 requests a year, in a priority queue, delivered within 10 working days as a spec added to the library. Requests outside the scope of workflow automation may be declined.

Free specs. Use them anywhere. Not for resale or republication.

The installer

The installer acts through the portal owner's own authorisation screen, with permissions limited to creating properties and workflows. Every workflow it creates arrives switched off. It never turns a workflow on, never deletes anything, and never stores the access token after the install finishes. What the API cannot create, such as a marketing email, a team or a static list, is listed in a task inside the portal instead.

Prices

Prices are shown excluding VAT and are one-time. Stripe, as merchant of record, adds the VAT or sales tax of your country at checkout and applies the EU reverse charge when you provide a valid VAT number. Founding prices until 31 October, the day the library passes 250 specs. Then 59 / 199 / 599 EUR.

Refunds

30 days, no questions asked. Email hello@theworkflowlibrary.com with your order number.

Updates

Specs added to a pack you bought are delivered through the same download link at no extra cost, every Friday until the catalogue is complete.

Accuracy

HubSpot changes its product. Each spec is dated and states what could not be verified at writing time. Report an inaccuracy and it is fixed within a week.

Privacy

Payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe is the merchant of record). Emails are sent from a marketing automation portal we operate, which also holds your contact record when you ask for the free specs or buy. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. Email hello@theworkflowlibrary.com to have your data deleted.
