What you get, and what you do not
    
Five sections written for you. Four left deliberately empty.

  

    
Written from the export

      
The trigger and its criteria in plain English, re-enrolment, every action in run order with its
        real values, the branches and their conditions, every property touched, and a diagram.

    
!
Marked TO WRITE

      
When to use it and when not to, the known pitfalls, the test plan, the variants. No export
        contains them. We leave them blank rather than invent them.

  

  
Measured on 2,060 real client workflows across
    nine portals: a fiche with both criteria and steps comes out for 76% of them, and 92% of
    all actions can be named. The four empty sections are 44% of a hand-written fiche by word
    count, which is the honest size of the gap between a copy and a spec.






  
Why it runs here and not on a server
    
A client's automation is not ours to hold.

    
An agency dropping an export is handing over a client's business logic. Uploading that
      to get a document back is a trade nobody should accept, so we did not build it that way. There is no
      upload endpoint to trust, because there is no upload.

  
This page is also the honest version of the bigger product. The Vault keeps
    those copies dated, puts one back after someone breaks it, and moves them between portals.
