Before you sign up: the one thing we have not proved yet

Reading a portal is measured. Writing into a portal is live. What we have not done yet is run one workflow all the way round, out of one portal and into another, and compare it action by action against the original. Until we have that number we are not selling this, which is why the list is free and why there is no card on this page.

Two more things worth knowing. Our read test ran on a developer test account, where HubSpot opens features regardless of plan, so on a real portal expect this to need Marketing Hub Professional or above. And a workflow points at owners, teams and lists that exist only in the portal it came from, so a copy always needs those resolving at the other end.

If the round trip turns out to lose too much, we publish the number here and close the list rather than ship something that quietly does less than you think.
