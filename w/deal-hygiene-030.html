Library / Deal hygiene / deal-hygiene-030

Notify finance and ops on Closed Won for invoicing kickoff

Deal hygieneDealSales Hub ProfessionalSimple~20 min to buildFull spec

Outcome. Finance and operations get notified automatically the moment a deal closes won, so invoicing and provisioning start without waiting on a rep to loop them in.

When to use it, when not to

  • UseUse it as the finance-facing counterpart to deal-hygiene-023's CS handoff, both fire from the same Closed Won moment but route to different teams.
  • UseUse it when invoicing today depends on a rep remembering to forward the closed deal to finance, or a delayed invoice is a recurring complaint.
  • SkipDo not use it if invoicing is triggered from a separate system of record, a signed contract in an e-signature tool, or a purchase order. Pick one trigger as the source of truth to avoid a duplicate invoice being raised from two systems.
  • SkipDo not route sensitive discount or margin detail to a broad distribution list without checking with finance what they actually need in the notification.
