Library / Deal hygiene / deal-hygiene-037

Multi-threading check: single-contact deal flagged for stall risk

Deal hygieneDealSales Hub ProfessionalIntermediate~30 min to buildFull spec

Outcome. A mid-funnel deal still associated with only one contact gets flagged, so reps widen the relationship before a single champion going quiet takes the whole deal down with them.

When to use it, when not to

  • UseUse it on deals past initial qualification, where a single point of contact is a real risk, not on brand-new deals where one contact is normal.
  • UseUse it as a coaching signal for the rep, not an automatic block, multi-threading is a sales behavior, not something a workflow can do on the rep's behalf.
  • SkipDo not use it on inherently single-contact motions, for example a sole proprietor or a very small company where a second contact genuinely does not exist.
  • SkipDo not use it as the only signal for deal risk, pair it with deal-hygiene-022's combined at-risk flag once both are running.
