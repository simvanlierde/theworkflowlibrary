New lead auto-assignment by owner rotation

Lead routing Sales Hub Professional Contact Simple ~20 min to build

Outcome. Every new contact gets an owner within minutes, spread evenly across the sales team, with zero territory or scoring logic involved.

Workflow mapEnrollment, branches and actions at a glance
flowchart TD
    A[Contact created, owner unknown, not a customer] --> B[Rotate to owner: New lead rotation pool]
    B --> C[Set lead routed date]
    C --> D[Notify new owner by internal email]

When to use it / when not to

  • Use it as the baseline routing workflow when the whole team sells to the same pool of leads, no territory or product split.
  • Use it as a starting point before layering territory, score, or product-based branching on top (see lead-routing-001, lead-routing-013).
  • Do not use it if any segment of leads needs a fixed owner (existing customers, named accounts, partner referrals). Exclude those first.
  • Do not use it if the team wants an SLA timer or Slack alert bundled in. That is a separate concern, see lead-routing-003 and lead-routing-004, and can be added as extra actions once this base workflow works.

What the full spec adds

  • Prerequisites: 5 items, every property with its internal name, type and options
  • Enrollment: trigger, criteria as ticked in the UI, re-enrollment rule, suppression
  • Steps: 3 numbered actions with their parameters, branches and delays
  • Known pitfalls: 4 traps seen in production
  • Test plan, Variants and the YAML build spec
  • Files: PDF, Markdown, properties JSON to create the fields in one API call
