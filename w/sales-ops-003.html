Create SDR-to-AE handoff task when a meeting is booked

Sales ops Sales Hub Professional Contact Simple ~25 min to build

Outcome. The moment an SDR-booked discovery meeting lands on an AE's calendar, the AE gets a task carrying the context they need (who booked it, why, what the SDR learned) instead of walking in cold.

Workflow mapEnrollment, branches and actions at a glance
flowchart TD
    A[Meeting Scheduled, Booked by is known] --> B[Set Handoff date]
    B --> C[Create handoff prep task for AE, includes SDR notes]
    C --> D[Notify AE by internal email with link and notes]
    D --> E{SDR notes blank?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Remind SDR to log notes before the meeting]
    E -->|No| G[End]

When to use it / when not to

  • Use it when SDRs qualify and book meetings on behalf of AEs (SDR owns the contact until handoff, AE owns the meeting and the deal going forward).
  • Use it when your team has had recurring "the AE didn't know why this meeting was booked" complaints.
  • Do not use it if the same person books and takes the meeting (no handoff needed, this workflow only makes sense when two different reps are involved).
  • Do not use it for meetings booked directly by the AE with their own contacts; only trigger it for SDR-originated bookings, see the enrollment filter below.

What the full spec adds

  • Prerequisites: 4 items, every property with its internal name, type and options
  • Enrollment: trigger, criteria as ticked in the UI, re-enrollment rule, suppression
  • Steps: 5 numbered actions with their parameters, branches and delays
  • Known pitfalls: 4 traps seen in production
  • Test plan, Variants and the YAML build spec
  • Files: PDF, Markdown, properties JSON to create the fields in one API call
