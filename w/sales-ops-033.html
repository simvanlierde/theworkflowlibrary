Surface a competitive battlecard task when a competitor name is logged in a deal note

Sales opsDealSales Hub EnterpriseAdvancedInstalls in one clickFull spec

Outcome. The moment a rep logs a note mentioning a known competitor, they get a task with a direct link to that competitor's battlecard, instead of digging through a shared drive mid-deal.

When to use it, when not to

  • UseUse it when your team maintains a maintained list of competitors with a battlecard link for each, and reps log discovery and call notes on the deal record.
  • UseUse it when the current process is "ask in Slack if anyone has faced this competitor," which is slow and inconsistent.
  • SkipDo not use it if competitor mentions mostly happen in call transcripts or emails rather than typed notes; this fiche only scans note text, not other engagement types (see Variants).
  • SkipDo not use it without an Enterprise-tier subscription; this fiche depends on a custom object to hold the competitor list and battlecard links, and custom objects require Sales Hub, Service Hub, or Data Hub Enterprise (https://knowledge.hubspot.com/object-settings/create-custom-objects).
